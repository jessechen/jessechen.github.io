title: Restrictions breed creativity
date: 2024-11-21
categories: human
---
Restrictions breed creativity. It's much easier to figure out where to start working on a thing that isn't quite what you want it to be, than a blank page. Prompts are one way people acknowledge this problem. By limiting the problem space with a prompt, you subtly cue up new ideas and unexpected solutions. Many people who work in creative areas have a prompt deck they'll draw from for inspiration if they're feeling stuck. Much like flipping a coin to make a trivial decision, you don't even have to follow the restriction or prompt that comes up as long as it successfully sparks a new idea.
