Out of curiosity, I put together a list of some ISO standards that seem to come up regularly when programming and what year they were first put into practice. These are in rough order of frequency.

* [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) (1988): Date and time format
* [ISO 3166](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2) (1974): Country codes
* [ISO 639](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ISO_639_language_codes) (1937): Language codes
* [ISO 4217](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_4217) (1978): Currency codes
* [ISO 9660](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_9660) (1988): Disk image format, aka .iso files
* [ISO 8859](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO/IEC_8859) (1987): Text encodings, e.g. Latin-1
