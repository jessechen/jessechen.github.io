I'm delighted to be invited to [Inkhaven, a writer's retreat for bloggers](https://www.inkhaven.blog/). I'll be writing onsite in Berkeley, CA for the month of April. They take an iron blogger approach to improving as a writer, so I've made a new blog category for Inkhaven posts. As such, I will either be posting daily, or be the first person ever kicked out of the retreat for not posting daily.

To borrow [a phrase from the Recurse Center](https://www.recurse.com/faq), my goal for the month is to become a dramatically better writer. I know I will necessarily struggle and flail on the way, and I hope you find the end result to be worth the temporary disruption.
