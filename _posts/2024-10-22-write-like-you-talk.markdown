title: Write more like you talk
date: 2024-10-22
categories: human
---
Most people's writing would improve if they wrote more like they talked. You probably learned how to write in an academic setting, and practice most of your writing in a similarly formal setting. While you've probably had plenty of casual conversations in text, often people don't consider that "real writing" and try not to let it affect their writing style. But you've got so much experience breaking things down and clearly conveying them in casual speech that could transfer to your writing with a change of mindset.
