[The world is more complicated than you think, even if you already think the world is really complicated.](http://johnsalvatier.org/blog/2017/reality-has-a-surprising-amount-of-detail) The linked example doesn't even begin to touch on the complexity created by things that are alive, let alone things that are conscious. I feel like this belief is something that's easy to memorize and recite, but much harder to really *know* in your bones.
